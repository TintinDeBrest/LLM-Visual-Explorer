# **LLM Visual Explorer — Exploring the Geometry of Meaning**

**LLM Visual Explorer (LlmExpl)** is a small laboratory for exploring how different models represent words and concepts in spaces with hundreds of dimensions.

It currently offers **more than 30 scenarios**, in **French, English and Spanish**, which can be explored with **4 different models**. The ultimate goal is to develop, through exploration, a **solid intuition** about how these models represent language.

## Seeing semantic space

It is impossible to directly see hundreds of dimensions.

LlmExpl therefore uses **2D maps** and a **3D planetarium**: a kind of “keyhole” through which we can glimpse this geometry. **Similarities** then allow us to measure relationships in the original space.

## Under the hood: surprises

The resulting geometry is **not necessarily organized according to our human intuition**.

It depends on the model, the language and the words we choose: concepts that seem intuitively close may move apart, while **surprising relationships** may emerge.

We start with a scenario suggested by P. Grim in *Understanding Artificial Intelligence*, and then continue with an extension of that scenario showing how much **the language and the model can change the result**.

## And before meaning?

The model does not directly receive “concepts”, but text divided into **tokens**.

We will finish by looking at tokenization, and then at how **context** can change the representation of ambiguous words such as *avocat*, *mouse* or *banco*.

> **See → explore → compare → measure → question**
